About

Curious first, then useful.

I'm a student who likes understanding how something works, then building a version of my own. Right now I'm studying prompt engineering: how to write instructions that make a model plan, reason and finish a task the way I meant it.

I'm a vibe coder. I describe what I want in plain language, let AI write the first draft of the code, then test it, read it and fix it until it works. That is how this site and my small agents get built.

Right now I'm also learning to trade. I'm starting with the basics: how markets work, how to read price charts, and above all how to manage risk so one bad trade can't wipe me out. I practice on paper before I think about real money, and I keep a journal of every trade and the reason I took it. Vibe coding helps here too, because I build small tools to track and test my ideas.

Alongside that I edit video. Cutting footage taught me rhythm and timing, and how to hold someone's attention. I carry that habit into everything I make.
